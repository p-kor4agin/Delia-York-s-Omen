After her ninth birthday, Delia sets out on an unusual side quest: getting to know her four classmates, discovering their interests, and finding the perfect gifts on the streets of York.

But under Chris Ratfield's watchful eye, things are never quite as simple as they seem.

With unexpected encounters, mysterious characters, bizarre situations, and Eve Lyne standing in her way, Delia's little adventure soon becomes something much stranger.

Welcome to **Delia: York's Omen**.
